# Article voice guideline update - 2026-05-07

For public reports and newsletters, do not expose the working process inside the article body.

Avoid phrases such as:
- "專家確認"
- "專家表格"
- "使用者提供"
- "一般讀者可以怎麼理解"
- "我們討論過"
- "審稿註記"
- "外部專家回饋指出"

Preferred style:
- Write as a finished report, not as a collaboration memo.
- Integrate expert input into the reporting logic without naming the review process unless the article is explicitly about expert commentary.
- Use direct industry-language sentences, then explain them naturally.
- Keep the tone warm and readable, not cold, bureaucratic, or checklist-like.
- Do not include internal source-processing notes such as transcript quality, draft status, or publication workflow in public copy.
